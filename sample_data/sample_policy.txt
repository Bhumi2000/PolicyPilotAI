=====================================================================
FICTIONAL SAMPLE POLICY - "ShopEasy" (for demonstration only)
=====================================================================
This policy is entirely fictional and is used only to demonstrate the
PolicyPilot AI prototype. It is not legal or commercial advice.

1. REFUND POLICY
Customers may request a full refund within 7 days of delivery. To be
eligible, the item must be unused and returned in its original packaging.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business
days after the returned item passes inspection. Shipping charges are
refunded only when the return is caused by an error on our side.

2. REPLACEMENT POLICY
Damaged, defective or faulty items reported within 10 days of delivery are
eligible for a free replacement of the same product. The customer must
provide a clear photograph of the damage and the order id. Replacements are
shipped within 3 business days of approval and are subject to stock
availability. If the same product is out of stock, a full refund is offered
instead of a replacement.

3. RETURN POLICY FOR LARGE APPLIANCES
Large appliances (for example refrigerators and washing machines) may be
returned within 10 days of delivery only if they are unused and still in
the sealed packaging. If the appliance was installed and then found faulty,
the warranty process in section 6 applies instead of a standard return.

4. DELIVERY POLICY
Standard delivery takes 4 to 6 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2
business days. A delivery is considered delayed when it exceeds the promised
date by more than 7 days. For delays beyond 15 days, customers may choose
between a full refund and a replacement. Compensation for ordinary delays is
not offered unless the delay exceeds 15 days.

5. CANCELLATION POLICY
Orders may be cancelled free of charge before they are shipped. Once an order
has been shipped it cannot be cancelled; the customer must instead follow the
return policy after delivery. Digital products and gift cards cannot be
cancelled or refunded once the code has been revealed.

6. WARRANTY POLICY
Electronics carry a 12-month manufacturer warranty covering manufacturing
defects. The warranty does not cover physical damage, liquid damage or damage
caused by misuse. Warranty claims require the order id and a service report
from an authorised centre.

7. EXCEPTIONS AND HUMAN REVIEW
Requests that fall outside the windows in sections 1 to 6 are exceptions and
require review and approval by a support manager. Support agents must not
promise refunds, replacements, compensation or delivery dates that are not
supported by this policy. If the correct outcome is unclear, escalate the
case to a human reviewer instead of guessing.
